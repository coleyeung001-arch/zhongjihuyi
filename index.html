常见问题

点了话筒没反应，或者提示没有权限

打开 iPhone「设置 → App → 中几互译」，把「麦克风」和「语音识别」都打开，再回到 App 点「我说中文」或「对方说法语」。

点「播放法语」没有声音

先看手机侧面的静音开关和音量。还没有声音，到 App「设置 → 发音」换一个法语声音，点「试听」。

断网时整句翻不了

词库里的两千多句不用联网。词库里没有的整句，要先在有网时到 App「设置 → 离线翻译 → 整句翻译」点「下载」，在系统弹出的窗口里下载中文和法语；下好以后断网也能翻。

联网翻译怎么打开、关掉

App「设置 → 联网翻译 → 允许联网翻译」。默认是关的。打开时会先给你看说明，点了「同意并打开」才算打开。打开后，词库里没有、你点了「翻译」或对着话筒说完的句子，会发到中几互译的翻译服务器，再交给第三方大模型服务商翻译；还在打字的半句不会发。也可以不打开：遇到词库里没有的句子时，只选「只联网翻这一句」。详见隐私政策。

结果上写着「参考」是什么意思

这句词库里没有，App 先按词拼了一个大概的说法，可能不通顺。想要整句翻译，可以下载离线翻译，或者打开联网翻译。

怎么加自己常用的话

App「设置 → 我的词条与备份 → 添加词条」，填中文和法语。可以「导出」成文件备份，换手机后「导入」。导出的是自己的词条和已存译文；收藏和最近翻译只存在这台手机上，不在备份里。

怎么删除我的数据

收藏、最近翻译、词条都只存在手机上，删除 App 就全部删掉；也可以在 App 里逐项清空。联网翻译时服务器上会留存常用句（最多 3 个月）和译文缓存（每条最多 90 天）；想提前删掉和某句话有关的留存，发邮件告诉我们原文和大约哪天翻的。详见隐私政策第 4、6 条。

Frequently asked questions

The microphone button does nothing, or says permission is missing

Open iPhone Settings → Apps → 中几互译 and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition, then go back to the app and tap “我说中文” (I speak Chinese) or “对方说法语” (They speak French).

No sound when I tap “播放法语” (Play French)

Check the silent switch and the volume. If there is still no sound, pick another French voice in the app under “设置 → 发音” (Settings → Voice) and tap “试听” (Test).

Full sentences cannot be translated offline

The 2,400+ phrasebook sentences need no connection. For sentences outside the phrasebook, go online once, open “设置 → 离线翻译 → 整句翻译” (Settings → Offline translation) in the app, tap “下载” (Download) and download Chinese and French in the system sheet. After that, translation works without a connection.

How do I turn online translation on or off?

In the app: “设置 → 联网翻译 → 允许联网翻译” (Settings → Online translation → Allow). It is off by default. When you switch it on, a notice is shown first and it is enabled only after you tap “同意并打开” (Agree and turn on). When it is on, sentences that are not in the phrasebook and that you have confirmed (the “翻译” button, or a finished spoken sentence) are sent to the 中几互译 translation server and then to a third-party large language model provider for translation; unfinished text is not sent. You can also leave it off and choose “只联网翻这一句” (this sentence only) when a sentence is not in the phrasebook. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Favourites, recent translations and your own entries are stored only on the phone and are removed when you delete the app; you can also clear them inside the app. With online translation, the server keeps common sentences (3 months at most) and a translation cache (90 days at most per entry). To have everything related to a sentence deleted earlier, e-mail us the text of the sentence and the approximate date. See sections 4 and 6 of the privacy policy.
