常见问题

点了话筒没反应，或者提示没有权限

打开 iPhone「设置 → App → 中几互译」，把「麦克风」和「语音识别」都打开，再回到 App 点「我说中文」或「对方说法语」。

点「播放法语」没有声音

先看手机侧面的静音开关和音量。还没有声音，到 App「设置 → 发音」换一个法语声音，点「试听」。

断网时整句翻不了

词库里的两千多句不用联网。词库里没有的整句，要先在有网时到 App「设置 → 离线翻译 → 整句翻译」点「下载」，在系统弹出的窗口里下载中文和法语；下好以后断网也能翻。

联网翻译怎么打开、关掉

App「设置 → 联网翻译 → 允许联网翻译」。默认是关的。打开后，词库里没有的句子会发到中几互译的翻译服务器，再交给第三方大模型服务商翻译，详见隐私政策。

结果上写着「参考」是什么意思

这句词库里没有，App 先按词拼了一个大概的说法，可能不通顺。想要整句翻译，可以下载离线翻译，或者打开联网翻译。

怎么加自己常用的话

App「设置 → 我的词条与备份 → 添加词条」，填中文和法语。可以「导出」成文件备份，换手机后「导入」。

怎么删除我的数据

收藏、最近翻译、词条都只存在手机上，删除 App 就全部删掉；也可以在 App 里逐项清空。服务器上的留存和删除办法见隐私政策第 4、6 条。

Frequently asked questions

The microphone button does nothing, or says permission is missing

Open iPhone Settings → Apps → 中几互译 and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition, then go back to the app and tap “我说中文” (I speak Chinese) or “对方说法语” (They speak French).

No sound when I tap “播放法语” (Play French)

Check the silent switch and the volume. If there is still no sound, pick another French voice in the app under “设置 → 发音” (Settings → Voice) and tap “试听” (Test).

Full sentences cannot be translated offline

The 2,400+ phrasebook sentences need no connection. For sentences outside the phrasebook, go online once, open “设置 → 离线翻译 → 整句翻译” (Settings → Offline translation) in the app, tap “下载” (Download) and download Chinese and French in the system sheet. After that, translation works without a connection.

How do I turn online translation on or off?

In the app: “设置 → 联网翻译 → 允许联网翻译” (Settings → Online translation → Allow). It is off by default. When it is on, sentences that are not in the phrasebook are sent to the 中几互译 translation server and then to a third-party large language model provider for translation. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Favourites, recent translations and your own entries are stored only on the phone and are removed when you delete the app; you can also clear them inside the app. For data kept on the server and how to have it deleted, see sections 4 and 6 of the privacy policy.
